SEG printing FAQs

How much does SEG fabric printing cost in Singapore?

SEG fabric printing starts from S$45 per square metre. Price is calculated on the printed graphic area, so a 3 × 2.24 m wall graphic is about 6.7 sqm. The silicone edge is sewn on as part of the print. Email us your sizes and quantities for an exact quote including delivery in Singapore.

What is SEG (silicone edge graphics) printing?

SEG printing is dye-sublimation printing on stretch fabric, finished with a thin silicone strip sewn around the edge. The strip pushes into the groove of an aluminium SEG frame so the fabric pulls tight and wrinkle-free. It is used for backlit lightboxes, trade show booths, backdrops, counters and exhibition walls.

Can you print graphics to fit my existing SEG frame?

Yes. Send us the graphic size or the frame's outer size and profile, and we print replacement fabric to fit. Reprints are a low-cost way to refresh a lightbox or booth for a new campaign while keeping the same aluminium frames. Both backlit and front-lit (non-lit) fabric options are available.

What artwork files do you need for SEG printing?

Supply print-ready PDF, TIFF or high-resolution JPG at full size or to scale, in CMYK, with around 100–150 dpi at full size and bleed as advised for your frame. Keep text and logos away from the silicone edge. We check every file and send a proof for approval before printing.

Do you deliver SEG prints across Singapore?

Yes. Printed SEG fabric graphics are folded or rolled, packed and delivered anywhere in Singapore, ready to push into your frames. Tell us your event date when you enquire so we can plan production and delivery in time for your exhibition or install.
