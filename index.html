What is SEG fabric?

A printed fabric graphic with a thin silicone strip sewn round the edge. Push it into the frame's groove and it pulls tight, wrinkle-free.

Seamless

Edge-to-edge colour, no visible fixings.

Lightweight

Fabric folds small and ships easy.

Reusable

Keep the frame, swap the graphic.

Quick change

New campaign? New print. Minutes to fit.

Glows backlit

Made for LED lightboxes. Colours pop.
